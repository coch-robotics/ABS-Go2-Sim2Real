My Contributions Engineering & Integration

01 / ADAPT

Cross-Platform Adaptation

Adjusted the robot model, training configuration, and observation/action interfaces for the differences between Go1 and Go2.

02 / TRAIN

Simulation Training and Validation

Trained the Go2 policy in Isaac Gym, tested varied obstacle layouts, and refined simulation settings using real-robot feedback.

03 / DEPLOY

Real-World Deployment and Debugging

Integrated perception and state inputs, model inference, and joint control; debugged issues during sim-to-real transfer.
